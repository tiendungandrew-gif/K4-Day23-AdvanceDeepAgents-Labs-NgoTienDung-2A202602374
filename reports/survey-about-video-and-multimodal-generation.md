# Survey on Video and Multimodal Generation

## TL;DR
- Video generation leverages hybrid models to create diverse and contextually relevant outputs [1].
- Recent advancements indicate that diffusion models improve video quality significantly, matching high cinematic standards [2].
- Industries like e-commerce and education are witnessing transformative impacts due to the efficiency of multimedia generation techniques [3].

## Background
The field of video and multimodal generation describes the technology and techniques used to create videos from various inputs, including text, audio, and other videos. The integration of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and variational autoencoders (VAEs) has evolved into more sophisticated frameworks, enabling higher quality and more diverse outputs [1]. Groundbreaking works demonstrate the potential of AI in redefining video content creation [3].

## Foundational Concepts
Current techniques in video generation predominantly utilize hybrid models combining VAEs and GANs (Video Generation From Text) [1], alongside diffusion processes which have shown remarkable promise in high-quality video generation [2]. Researchers highlighted the importance of benchmarking methods to improve evaluation across video generation systems [4].

## State-of-the-Art Techniques
Leading techniques utilize diffusion models that generate videos from specific conditions, such as video-to-video or text-to-video frameworks [2][4]. Tools such as TC-Bench have been developed to systematically test models' temporal compositionality, revealing gaps and opportunities for enhancement [5]. These developments showcase the transition from simplistic models to complex generative systems capable of producing high-quality cinematic narratives [4]. 

## Applications and Impacts
The application of video generation technologies is extensive, ranging from e-commerce, which utilizes AI to produce effective marketing content rapidly, to healthcare [3]. Companies like Condé Nast leverage AI for video discovery and content management, significantly increasing operational efficiency [6]. These advancements demonstrate the growing significance of multimodal generation, as industries increasingly rely on integrated technologies to enhance audience engagement and streamline workflows [3][7].


## Trends and Open Problems
In recent years, there’s been a notable trend towards creating synchronized audiovisual experiences [8]. However, challenges remain regarding the quality and accuracy of generated content. The development of seamless multimodal systems is still in progress, necessitating further exploration into integrating audio with visual content to create truly immersive experiences [3].
