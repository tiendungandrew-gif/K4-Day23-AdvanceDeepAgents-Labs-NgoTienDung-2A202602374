# Survey about World Model

## TL;DR


- World models represent environments and are key to AI learning [1].
- Recent architectures emphasize generative models and multimodal integrations [2][3].
- Benchmarks evaluate world model applications, particularly in robotics [4][5].

## Background
World models can be defined as generative representations that encapsulate the dynamics of the environment, assisting various AI applications ranging from robotics to cognitive modeling [6]. Significant foundational papers trace the evolution of these models from classic cognitive theories to contemporary approaches that utilize advanced neural networks [7].

## Foundational Concepts
Recent investigations into foundational concepts of world models have highlighted historical developments, emphasizing cognitive models from neuroscience and their influence on AI principles. The key paper 'A Definition and Roadmap for World Models' outlines the necessity for actionable simulations rather than exhaustive reproductions of reality [8]. This shift emphasizes the importance of understanding state transitions in environments.

## Modern Architectures and Techniques
In modern architectures, recent advancements focus on generative models and hybridized approaches, which combine visual and language data for multi-task learning. For instance, the 'World Models' framework showcases a generative neural network that enhances reinforcement learning by simulating environments in a dream-like state [8]. Techniques like token-level routing also streamline processes in large language models, adding efficiency to inference tasks across these architectures [9].

## Benchmarks and Applications
Benchmarks such as PBench and WorldBench assess the effectiveness and fidelity of world models in practical applications, revealing insights into their performance in robotics and physical interaction scenarios [10][11]. These frameworks highlight the challenges faced, such as maintaining both physical accuracy and visual coherence, underscoring the need for refined metrics in model evaluation [12].

## Trends and Open Problems
The past two years have seen an ongoing evolution of world models characterized by rapid advancements in methodologies and applications. Challenges remain regarding improving fidelity in physical modeling and robustness in casual environments. While generative models have made strides in visual representation, gaps persist between simulated actions and their real-world counterparts [13][14].
